GLENPA REAL Executive Showcase | iPhone Home Screen Demo

1. Extract the ZIP archive.
2. On a computer, open https://app.netlify.com/drop and sign in if prompted.
3. Drag the extracted GLENPA_REAL_iPhone_Netlify_Ready folder onto the Netlify Drop page.
4. Wait for Netlify to provide a https://...netlify.app address.
5. Open that address on the iPhone in Safari.
6. Tap Share > Add to Home Screen > Add. The GLENPA REAL icon will appear.

IMPORTANT: The portal and every record are fictional presentation data.
The browser-only demo uses local storage; data entered on one device will not
synchronize to the MSL dashboard on another device. The built-in demo passwords
are not real authentication and are exposed in the HTML source. Do not enter
actual patient names, clinical information, or other confidential data.
The link remains accessible to anybody who possesses it unless you add real
access restrictions. Noindex is not an access control.
